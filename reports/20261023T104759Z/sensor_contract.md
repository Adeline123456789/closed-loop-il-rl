# Measured sensor/camera contract — 2026-10-26

The actor receives all six components of `force_sensor_world_smooth`; the variable name does not define the frame. PhysX 5.5.1 defines incoming joint force in the joint frame, parent acting on child, force in N and torque in Nm, averaged over the last physics step. This is a 120 Hz sampled substep signal, not an instantaneous collision peak. [Pinned PhysX API semantics](https://nvidia-omniverse.github.io/PhysX/physx/5.5.1/docs/Articulations.html#link-incoming-joint-force).

Actual USD inspection found `/World/envs/env_0/Robot/panda_link7/panda_hand_joint`, parent `panda_link7`, child `force_sensor`; child anchor local position (0,0,0) and rotation (1,0,0,0). Therefore the incoming joint frame coincides with this sensor link's local frame and origin. No world-frame claim or hole-origin torque translation is made. Signs are inherited from the documented parent-on-child convention; no unmeasured sign flip is inferred from the old HF card. The stock translated `force_sensor_smooth` is retained only as audit data and is not used by the actor or costs. Old HF wrench frame/reference/sign are still unverified.

The raw six-component samples, the stock filtered raw signal and the stock translated signal are recorded separately. Real 24-step probes verify alpha=0.25 smoothing once per 120 Hz physics update, zero maximum filter discrepancy. Nine boundary samples are captured per eight-substep control interval; the first repeats the previous boundary. Calibration uses only samples 1..8 to avoid duplicate weighting. Contact cost/hard protection read unnormalized raw samples, while learning history uses causal filtered values. No fictitious torque is appended to the three-component HF noisy-force field.

The fixed 256x256 RGB camera uses eye [0.95,0.45,0.45], target [0.6,0,0.12], focal length 24, horizontal aperture 20.955, clipping [0.1,10] in each environment frame. `contract_probe_02/camera.png` visibly contains the gripper, peg and hole; `expert_frame_*.png` show actual expert progression. This is a new same-version camera contract, not recovered HF extrinsics. USD cloning is enabled because the camera cannot enumerate Fabric-only clones; stock physics replication is retained.

Physical thresholds are the predeclared development-only empirical simulation operating envelope in `physical_calibration.json`, derived from eight completed successful development parents. They are not manufacturer damage limits or a hardware safety guarantee. Development parents are excluded from all ACT training/validation/test data. Hard protection is independent of learned Q and holds the measured end-effector pose after an exceeded raw threshold.
